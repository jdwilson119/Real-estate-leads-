What we do

Remediation and closure, delivered by people who do the work.

Consultants write the plan; we put it into practice. RemX brings the crews, plant and know-how to deliver remediation and closure projects safely, on budget and to the standard regulators expect.

Contaminated land remediation

Excavation, treatment and validated removal of impacted soils from industrial, commercial and residential sites, from hydrocarbons and heavy metals to PFAS and legacy fill.

Mine closure

Practical delivery of closure plans for mine sites and exploration areas, decommissioning infrastructure and making landforms safe, stable and non-polluting.

Land rehabilitation

Hazardous materials

Why RemX

A contractor that understands contamination.

Contaminated sites and closure works carry risk to people, the environment and your timeline. We plan carefully, communicate clearly and leave your site ready for what comes next.

  • Local and responsive. Based in Ascot, minutes from Perth Airport and the eastern industrial areas, with crews ready to go to regional sites.
  • We work with your consultant. We follow your environmental consultant's remediation action plan and support their validation.
  • Compliance is built in. Waste tracking, documentation and safety systems that hold up to client and regulator scrutiny.
  • Straightforward pricing. Clear scopes, honest variations and no surprises at the end of the job.
