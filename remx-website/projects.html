The kind of work we deliver

Every site is different, but the goal is the same: leave it safe, compliant and ready for its next use. Here are typical projects RemX delivers for clients across WA.

Contaminated land

Former service station remediation

Removing underground fuel tanks, excavating and disposing of hydrocarbon-impacted soil, and supporting validation so the site can be redeveloped.

Mine closure

Exploration camp & pad closure

Decommissioning infrastructure, removing waste, reshaping disturbed areas and preparing ground for revegetation at a remote WA site.

Rehabilitation

Drill pad & track rehabilitation

Ripping, respreading topsoil and seeding cleared exploration tracks and drill pads to support native vegetation regrowth.

Industrial

Legacy industrial site clean-up

Segregating mixed fill, managing asbestos-containing material with licensed partners, and removing legacy drums and chemical waste.

Development

Residential subdivision pre-works

Identifying and removing impacted fill before civil works start, keeping the developer's programme on track.

Water

Dewatering & water treatment support

Managing impacted groundwater encountered during excavation with temporary collection, treatment and containment.
