FAQ

Common questions

Do you work with our environmental consultant?

Yes. Most of our work follows a remediation action plan or closure plan prepared by an environmental consultant. We work closely with them on sampling, validation and reporting.

Do you work outside the Perth metro area?

Yes. We're based in Ascot and regularly plan works for regional and remote sites across WA, including mobilising plant, crews and accommodation.

Can you manage the disposal of contaminated waste?

We arrange classification, licensed transport and disposal at appropriately licensed facilities, and provide the waste tracking documentation you need for your records and for regulators.

How quickly can you start?

That depends on scope and approvals, but we aim to respond to every enquiry within one business day and can often mobilise quickly for urgent clean-ups.

How do you price jobs?

We can provide lump-sum prices for well-defined scopes, or schedule-of-rates pricing where volumes are uncertain, which is common with contaminated soils.

Got a site that needs attention?

Tell us what you're dealing with. We'll come back to you within one business day with practical next steps and a no-obligation quote.
