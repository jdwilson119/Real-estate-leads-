Practical people for complex sites

RemX Australia is a Western Australian environmental remediation and contracting business based in Ascot, Perth.

Our story

Built for remediation.

RemX Australia was set up to fill a gap between environmental consultants and general civil contractors: a team that knows earthworks and understands contamination, waste regulations and closure requirements.

From our base in Ascot, close to Perth Airport and the city's eastern industrial areas, we support clients across the metro area and out to regional and remote Western Australia. Our crews are trained for contaminated and hazardous environments, and our project managers keep consultants, clients and regulators informed at every stage.

We're growing, and we're investing in people, plant and systems to take on larger and more complex projects across Australia.

Our values

What you can expect from us

Safety without compromise

Every task is planned and risk-assessed. Nobody is asked to do work that isn't safe, and every person goes home safe at the end of the day.

Care for Country

We respect the land we work on and the communities around it, and we aim to leave every site better than we found it.

Doing what we say

Clear scopes, honest communication and a programme we stick to. When things change, you'll hear it from us first.

Quality you can sign off

Thorough records, waste tracking and close-out documentation, so your project is complete in the paperwork as well as on the ground.

Safety & compliance

Systems that stand up to scrutiny.

  • Project-specific safety, environmental and traffic management plans
  • Crews inducted and trained for contaminated and hazardous work
  • Waste classification, tracking and disposal records for every load
  • Work carried out in line with WA environmental and WHS legislation
